# ASCII sharks

Adapted from https://github.com/stackedlol/ascii-sharks by stackedlol.
Source revision: c521e24a787e6d4cc460d4cf2b9815f25e4fe453.

The upstream shark geometry, swimming simulation, and ASCII rasterizer are
retained. Portfolio changes render the sharks on a transparent canvas,
use translucent glyphs in the site's original light/dark ink with distinct great
hammerhead, thresher, and tiger silhouettes, tune speed and frame rate, omit fish,
pause while hidden or when reduced motion is requested, and preserve the
same sharks through resizing and navigation.

Fish-free roaming adds smoothly varying speed and broad, momentum-driven turns.
Each click reserves one shark for a smooth, direct approach to an ASCII portal.
The shark contracts into the ripple, disappears, then emerges elsewhere on
screen. Extra clicks queue without interrupting occupied portals. ASCII crests
stay alive until arrival, crossfade, and dissolve into punctuation. Journey
state persists across navigation and resize. No automatic
water-surface effects or shark trails are rendered.
